**Final Direction:**
Swiss Monochrome (Light Mode)
- **Background**: Pure white (`#ffffff`) or very light gray (`#fafafa`)
- **Text**: Pure black (`#000000`) and dark grays (`#666666`)
- **Typography**: Inter (or Geist if available) for headings/body, and JetBrains Mono for data labels and numbers.
- **Borders**: Sharp 1px borders (`#eaeaea`), no rounded corners or extremely subtle (2-4px).
- **Cards**: Flat white with crisp borders, subtle hover state (background turns slightly gray).
- **Buttons**: Inverted black-on-white or white-on-black.
